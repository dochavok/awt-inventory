# AWT Inventory: Questions for the DMs

**For:** the AWT DMs, and the game's owner (Q10).
**From:** Craig, for the AWT Inventory app.

There's no rulebook yet, so a few rules the app needs to follow are unclear. Each question below says why the app needs to know. Short answers are fine. Until a question is answered, the app either leaves the rule out or only warns.

Answered questions stay here with their answer, so their numbers never change.

## Equipment and hands

**Q1. Does a two-handed weapon need the Dual Wielding skill?**
Dual Wielding lets a character equip two one-handed weapons. A two-handed weapon is roughly as strong as two one-handed weapons, so allowing it without the skill could be a way round that rule. Can any character with Weapon Use equip a two-handed weapon, or only one with Dual Wielding?

**Q2. Can a character equip a shield in each hand?**
Shields normally go in the off hand. Is there anything stopping a character from using two shields?

**Q3. Can a character have a bow and a melee weapon equipped at the same time?**
For example, a bow on one hand and a sword on the other. Should the app allow it, or is there a rule against it?

**Q4. How do consumables and quickslots work?**
- Must a consumable be in a quickslot to be used in combat without tasking?
- How many quickslots does a character start with?
- Which containers give quickslots? Do some only take certain kinds of consumable? (For example, the Poisoner's Pouch skill gives one quickslot for consumables, or two for poisons.)
- Does a quickslot container take up a paper-doll slot, such as Belt?

## Rarity and arcane items

**Q5. What level is needed to equip each rarity?**
What character level must a character reach to equip an Epic, Legendary, Divine or Mythic item?

**Q6. What is a "DM Arcane" item?**
How is it different from a regular arcane item? Does it need an arcane slot?

**Q7. Are there limits on Divine and Mythic items?**
Only one Legendary item can normally be equipped at a time.
- Is there a similar limit for Divine or Mythic items?
- Does a Divine or Mythic item count toward the Legendary limit?
- Is there an order, such as Epic < Legendary < Divine < Mythic?

## Skills

**Q9. Which weapon groups does Weapon Focus recognise?**
Weapon Focus gives a bonus "with a specific weapon group". Is a group a single weapon type (sword) or a wider family (blades, covering swords and daggers)? Is there an official list?

## Familiars, allies and minions

**Q11. What are the rules for familiars, allies and minions?**
The app lets a player record a character's familiars and allies, mark them *active*, and count what they add (such as CM, hearts or an extra combat die).
- What's the difference between a familiar, an ally, a minion and a summon? Which of them belong in a character's records, and which only exist during a combat?
- How many can a character have active at once? Is there a limit for each kind?
- Do an ally's bonuses always count, or only in some situations?
- Can allies have their own equipment? (For example, the Undead Armor skill lets a Skeleton or Ghoulhulk equip a weapon and a piece of armor.) If so, does that equipment use the character's slots, or the ally's own?

## For the game's owner

**Q10. May the app show the game's skill text and use its level chart, and may they be published in the app's public code repository?**
The app lets players pick their skills and spells from the game's list, and it would show each one's card text. It also needs the level chart (class bonus, combat dice, hearts and arcane slots by level) to work out each character's totals. Both belong to the game. The app's code is public on GitHub, under a licence that says the game's content isn't covered by it and still belongs to you.
- May the app show the skill and spell card text to its users?
- May the skill and spell text be kept in the public repository?
- May the level chart's numbers be kept in the public repository?

Until you answer, none of this goes into the public repository.
