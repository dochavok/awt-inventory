# History

reviewed: 2026-11-12

Each character has its own history, newest first: open **History** from the character's page. It records:

- entries **acquired**, with where they came from;
- entries **used**, and uses **restored**;
- entries **traded** and **given away**;
- **level changes**;
- **class corrections**.

Each record has its date, the entry it's about, a quantity where one applies, and an optional note. When an entry leaves the inventory, its record keeps a copy of the entry as it was, so the record still reads properly once the entry is gone.

## Dates

A record's date is today unless you choose an earlier one, for example when catching up after a faire weekend. The record also keeps when it was actually entered. Dates are shown in your phone's own time.

## Filtering

The history can be filtered by event and by category, with the two lists at the top.

## Restoring a use

A use marked by mistake has a **Restore** button in the history. It brings the quantity back up by one, and the history records the restore next to the use. Each use can be restored once.

## Links

A record's **Link**, such as to the post in Discord's rewards channel, opens in a new tab.

## Records can't be edited

History records can't be changed or removed one at a time. They go only when you delete their entry or the character.
