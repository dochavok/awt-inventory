# Getting started

reviewed: 2026-11-12

AWT Inventory keeps your Adventures from the Wizards Tower characters' records on your phone: what they own, what's equipped, their skills, titles and history. It's your own record, not an official one.

## Creating an account

Until release 1.0 the app is invite-only: the maintainer adds your email address to the list first. Anyone else sees "not open yet".

1. Open the app and choose **Create an account**.
2. Choose a **display name**. It's the only thing about you other players ever see, and only if you list something for trade. No real name is needed.
3. Sign up with an email address and password, or with your Google account.
4. Read and accept the [terms of use](terms-of-use.md). The [privacy note](privacy.md) says what's stored and who can see it.
5. With an email address, open the link the app emails you, then choose **I've verified it**. Until your email is verified, nothing can be saved to your account.

## Signing in

On your phone you stay signed in until you sign out, so you never have to sign in at the faire. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen and follow the email.

## Adding the app to your home screen

Added to the home screen, the app opens full screen with its own icon.

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Do this: Safari can clear a website's stored data after about 7 days without a visit, unless the site is on the home screen.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the menu and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Your account

**Account**, at the top of every screen, is where you change your display name, sign out, read the privacy note, or delete your account. Deleting it removes everything in it for good: type your display name to confirm.

## Without signal

Open the app once with signal, and everything in your account is kept on your phone. At the faire, without signal, you can still open every character and make changes. A bar at the top says when you're offline and how many changes are waiting; they're sent as soon as there's signal again.

Signing in on a new phone and creating an account need signal.

If the server refuses a change once it's sent, the app says which one and undoes it on your phone. That happens, for example, when two phones each add a character with the same name while offline: the first to get signal keeps it. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Help

**Help**, at the top of every screen, opens the page about that screen. The help works without signal too. Each page says when it was last updated and when it was last checked against the app.
