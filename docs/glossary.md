# Glossary

reviewed: 2026-11-12

The words the app uses, and what they mean in it.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **User** | Someone with an account: a player of AWT. |
| **Maintainer** | Whoever looks after the app and its game data. The maintainer has no special screens in the app. |
| **Character** | One of your AWT characters, with a name, a class (Warrior, Mage or Rogue) and a level. You can have any number. |
| **Retired character** | A character you've stopped playing. Hidden from your main list, shown under *Retired*, kept whole, and can be brought back. |
| **Category** | One of the seven inventory sections: Weapons, Armor, Skills, Familiars/Allies, Consumables, Items, Components. |
| **Entry** | One thing a character has in a category, such as a sword, a potion or a skill. |
| **Equipped / in use** | A weapon or piece of armor in a slot, or an arcane item in an arcane slot. An *active* ally counts the same way. |
| **Paper doll** | The character's equipment slots: Head, Chest and the rest, plus Main hand and Off hand. Each piece of armor fits one kind of slot. |
| **Arcane item / arcane slot** | An arcane item is in use only while it's in an arcane slot, and never takes a paper-doll slot. The number of arcane slots comes from class and level, plus grants. |
| **Rarity** | An optional label on an item: Epic, Legendary, Divine or Mythic. Some rarities limit how many can be equipped at once. |
| **Allowance** | How many items of a rarity a character may have equipped at once. |
| **Permission** | Whether a character may equip a kind of thing: Weapon Use, Ranged Weapon Use, Armor Use, Dual Wielding. |
| **Grant** | Something a source adds to a character: CM, mitigation, hearts, an extra die, extra slots, an allowance, a permission, or an effect such as a resistance. |
| **Source** | What a grant comes from: an entry (only while equipped or active), a skill, a title, or a manual grant with a reason. |
| **Situational** | A grant with a condition, such as "vs Evil". Listed with its source, never added to a total. |
| **Class bonus** | Combat Bonus (Warrior), Arcane Bonus (Mage) or Stealth Bonus (Rogue), from the level chart. Part of CM, and shown on its own. |
| **CM** | Combat modifier: the class bonus plus every CM grant that counts. Shown with where it comes from. |
| **Mitigation** | Hearts of damage taken off each hit. Grants from every source add up. |
| **Combat dice** | The dice a character rolls in combat: the level chart's, plus extra dice from grants, each with its source. |
| **Title** | An honour from the game, with a name and a description. Applies to all your characters unless you make it one character's. Patreon tiers are titles too. |
| **Skill list** | The game's skills and spells, which skills are picked from, never typed in. |
| **Item family** | An optional group an item belongs to by origin or theme, across categories. Typed by you, with suggestions. |
| **Weapon group** | The kind of weapon, such as Sword, Dagger or Bow. |
| **Component tier** | A crafting component's tier, usually Minor, Major, Epic or Legendary, but typed by you. Unrelated to rarity. |
| **Used** | A consumable or component spent in play. Its quantity drops by one; it can be restored. |
| **Traded** | An entry traded or given away. It leaves the inventory and the history keeps a copy. |
| **Hard delete** | Removing an entry added by mistake. It leaves no trace in the history. |
| **History** | A character's dated record of entries gained, used, traded and restored, and of level and class changes. Records can't be edited. |
| **Over-limit alert** | A notice on a character with more equipped than it's now allowed, because a grant was lost. |
| **Reference data** | The game's fixed lists the app works from (the level chart, slot kinds, rarities and the skill list), and the common suggestions for fields you type. |
| **Quickslot** | A game feature still being worked out with the DMs. Not in the app yet. |
| **DM Arcane** | A game feature still being worked out with the DMs. Until then, a DM Arcane item is recorded as an ordinary arcane item, with a note. |
