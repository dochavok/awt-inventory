# Characters and titles

reviewed: 2026-11-12

## Your characters

**Characters**, at the top of every screen, lists your characters by name, with each one's class and level. There's no limit on how many you can have. Characters you've retired are listed under **Retired**. **Search all characters** looks through all of their inventories at once: see [Search and export](search-and-export.md).

### Adding a character

Choose **Add a character**, then give its name, class (Warrior, Mage or Rogue) and level, and any notes. Your characters must have different names: "corvin" and "Corvin" count as the same name. Other players can use a name you use.

### Editing

On a character's page, choose **Edit** to change its name, level or notes. Every change of level is recorded in the character's history, for example "Level 12 → 13".

A character's page also links to each category of its inventory, its history and **Search**, and **Print or export** makes its printable sheet: see [Search and export](search-and-export.md).

### Correcting the class

The game has no way to change class, so **Correct the class** is only for fixing a mistake made when the character was created. The app warns you first and says what the change affects: the class bonus, combat dice, hearts, arcane slots, and which skill trees are the character's own. Tick **I understand this fixes a mistake** to go ahead. The correction is recorded in the history, for example "Class corrected: Rogue → Warrior".

### Retiring and bringing back

**Retire** a character you've stopped playing. It moves to the **Retired** list, and nothing in it changes. Open it there and choose **Bring back** to return it to your main list.

### Deleting a character

**Delete this character**, at the bottom of its page, removes the character for good, with everything in it, its history, and titles that are only its own. Type the character's name exactly to confirm. To stop playing a character but keep it, retire it instead.

## Titles

Titles are honours from the game, each with a name, a description and the date earned. Patreon tiers are recorded as titles too; the app has no link to Patreon.

Open **Titles** at the top of the screen to add one. The description is optional, but worth adding: the list shows it.

### Whose title it is

A new title applies to **all your characters**, including ones you create later. To make it one character's, choose **Edit** and set **Applies to** to that character. You can switch it back to all characters at any time. A character's page lists every title that applies to it.

### Editing and removing

Choose **Edit** on a title to change its name, description, date or who it applies to, or **Remove** to remove it.
