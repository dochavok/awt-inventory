# Terms of use

reviewed: 2026-11-12

- Accounts are for ages 13 and up.
- This app is your own record, not an official one. The DMs and the game's rules decide what a character has.
- Trades are arranged and carried out between players, outside the app. The app only shows what's listed.
- No offensive display names or listing text, and no listings for things you don't have. The maintainer may remove content or accounts that break this.
- The app is provided as is. It may change or shut down, and data could be lost, so keep your own export of your records.
- Deleting your account is permanent: everything in it is removed and can't be brought back.
